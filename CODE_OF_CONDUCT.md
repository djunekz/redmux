# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in REDMUX welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or technical ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements with evidence.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on the work rather than the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and abusive language are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may restrict participation when necessary. Reports should be sent privately to the repository maintainer using the contact method on the repository profile.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and community interactions related to REDMUX.
